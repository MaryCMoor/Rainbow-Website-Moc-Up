OUR FOUNDATION & COMMITMENT

Who We Are

The International Order of the Rainbow for Girls (IORG) is a nonprofit, service-oriented organization that teaches girls ages 11-20 leadership through community service. Since our founding, we have empowered young women to develop confidence, citizenship, and character through involvement in local assemblies across New Jersey and beyond.

Our members participate in service projects, learn about parliamentary procedure, and gain valuable life skills that prepare them for success in school, careers, and life. We foster an inclusive environment where every girl is encouraged to find her voice and make a difference.

Girls serving their community with pride and purpose.

Our Core Values

These six pillars guide everything we do, shaping our members into compassionate leaders and dedicated servants.

Leadership

Developing confidence and skills to lead with integrity.

Service

Serving others with compassion and humility.

Friendship

Building lifelong bonds of sisterhood and support.

Faith

Nurturing spiritual growth and personal belief.

Hope

Inspiring optimism and resilience for the future.

Charity

Giving generously to those in need.
