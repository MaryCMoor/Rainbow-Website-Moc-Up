Who We Are

The International Order of the Rainbow for Girls is a nonprofit youth organization that teaches girls what matters most – leadership, confidence, and citizenship. Every day, across the globe, hundreds of thousands of girls come together to laugh, share, and grow through fun events and challenging workshops designed to empower girls of all ages.

Rainbow Girls volunteer for their community and travel to other clubs. Along the way, they make new friends and gather invaluable life skills that help them become the best daughter, sister, and friend they can be. Whether their dream is to become the CEO of their own company, a talented artist, or a loving mother, Rainbow Girls learn that they can accomplish anything, and in the process, they can make a difference in this world.

Our Core Values

👑

Leadership

We develop confident leaders who know how to inspire others, make difficult decisions, and create positive change in their communities and beyond.

🤝

Service

We put our faith into action by serving others, volunteering in our communities, and learning the joy of helping those in need.

💖

Friendship

We build lifelong friendships that span generations, creating a sisterhood of support, encouragement, and shared experiences.

✨

Faith • Hope • Charity

We nurture spiritual growth, maintain optimism in challenging times, and practice generosity of time, talent, and treasure to make the world a better place.
