Rainbow Pledge (Ages 5-10)

The lessons taught through the Rainbow Pledge program teach moral values, teamwork, respect, patriotism, and charity to others. Rainbow builds character, self-confidence, friendship and faith.

What is Rainbow Pledge?

Rainbow Pledges are girls age 5 to 10 years old. Each pledge Group is affiliated with a Rainbow Assembly. Pledge Groups typically meet twice a month: one formal and one informal meeting. At their formal meeting, the girls wear white dresses, or skirts and blouses. They conduct their business and perform their ceremonies. At their informal meeting, the Pledge Mother or Pledge Sister plans an activity, an outing, or a charity or service project. Rainbow Pledges may earn merit bars for such things as regular attendance, participation in charity or service projects, and holding offices.

The New Jersey Rainbow Pledge Program is part of the International Order of the Rainbow for Girls (IORG). We are only affiliated with Masonic Lodges, Order of the Eastern Star Chapters, and Courts of Amaranth, however a girl does not need to be affiliated with one of these groups for membership.

How to Join

To find out more about our Pledge Groups, contact the web master.

Requirements to petition for membership in the organization are:

  • A desire to want to belong to The International Order of the Rainbow for Girls.
  • If elected to membership, a promise to adhere to the Supreme Assembly's Constitution and Statutes, and a cheerful compliance to the By-Laws, Rules, and Regulations of the Assembly to whom she makes the petition.
  • Be a girl of good moral character.
  • Be a believer in one true and living God.
  • Be an unmarried girl between the ages of 5 and 10 for a Pledge Assembly, or 11 and 20 for a Rainbow Assembly.
  • Be either the daughter or granddaughter of a Master Mason, member of Eastern Star or Majority Rainbow Girl, or be a friend of a Rainbow Girl.
  • Never have petitioned and been rejected for the degrees in any Rainbow Assembly of the International Order of the Rainbow for Girls.
  • Be recommended by two Rainbow girls and be sponsored by either a Master Mason, member of the Order of the Eastern Star, member of the Order of the Amaranth, or a majority Rainbow Girl. This adult will also sign her petition. Your local assembly can help you find the girl & adult sponsors.

Upon meeting the above requirements, contact a local Assembly of the International Order of the Rainbow for Girls, Masonic Lodge, Order of the Eastern Star, or Order of the Amaranth. If you don't find one or they don't know of an Assembly locally check our Assemblies Page, or e-mail us at one of the links on this web site.
