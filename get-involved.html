The Rainbow Pledge

I promise to pray, to worship, and to serve, to honor my parents and elders, to be kind and helpful to others, to be obedient and courteous, to be honest and truthful, to be loyal to my friends and my country, to be pure in thought and deed, and to strive at all times to live by the teachings of the Bible and the lessons of the Rainbow.

This pledge guides our actions and shapes our character as Rainbow Girls.
