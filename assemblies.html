About Our Assemblies

The International Order of the Rainbow for Girls is organized into local groups called Assemblies. Each Assembly meets regularly in their community to develop leadership, participate in service projects, and build lifelong friendships. With Assemblies throughout New Jersey, there's sure to be one near you!

Each Assembly has its own unique identity, including a theme, motto, colors, and service projects. Click on any Assembly card below to learn more about that specific group.
